AI BASED VTOL UAV (Vertical Take-Off and Landing) Unmanned Aerial Vehicle engineered for critical mission profiles, including AI-driven border monitoring, natural disaster survivor detection, and fully autonomous navigation in GPS-denied environments.

🚀 Overview
The Stallion VTOL is built for compactness, modularity, and extended endurance. Designed around a robust V-tail configuration and dual tractor motors, this platform accommodates advanced edge-computing payloads to process complex computer vision tasks in real-time. Whether conducting continuous border surveillance or navigating satellite-denied zones using sensor fusion, the Stallion VTOL delivers high-performance reliability.

✨ Key Features
AI-Based Surveillance & Detection: Employs onboard edge computing hardware and OpenCV-driven computer vision to autonomously detect anomalies across borders and identify survivors during natural disaster relief operations.
GPS-Denied Autonomy: Integrates precise sensor fusion combining LiDAR and Optical Flow data, enabling safe, stable autonomous flight in environments where satellite signals are compromised or unavailable.
Modular Payload Architecture: The fully detachable nose section allows for rapid swapping of various sensors, standard 19x19mm FPV cameras, and AI processing hardware.
High Efficiency & Endurance: Capable of sustained stable flight at just under 40% throttle, achieving flight times exceeding 4 hours when equipped with a high-capacity 21Ah Li-Ion battery pack.

🛠️ Technical Specifications
General Airframe Data
Wingspan: 1340mm
Length: 990mm
Wing Area: 26.5 dm²
Airfoil: Eppler E205
Center of Gravity: 60mm from the leading edge at the wing root
All-Up Weight (AUW): 1500g to 3000g
Optimal Cruise Speed: 60-70 km/h
Recommended Hardware Configuration
Motors: T-Motor F60 1750KV or T-Motor F90 1300KV
Propellers: 7x4, 7x5, or 7x6 (configured as one CW and one CCW)
Flight Controller: Speedybee F405 Wing or equivalent Mavlink-compatible FC
ESC: 2x BlHeliS 40A
Servos: 4x Corona 929MG Metal Gear or similar
Battery Requirements: 4S (up to 4S6P 21Ah Li-Ion) for endurance; 3S configurations supported for lightweight operations
GPS: Matek M10Q or equivalent compass-equipped unit
Receiver: Matek R24-D ELRS or similar


🏗️ Structural Components
The airframe utilizes a highly reinforced carbon spar system to ensure rigidity under varied flight loads:
Main Wing Spar: 10x800mm Carbon Tube
Secondary Wing Spars: 2x 8x600mm Carbon Tubes
Wing Spar Extensions: 2x 6x430mm Carbon Tubes
Tail Boom: 16x435mm Carbon Tube
V-Tail Spars: 4x 4x260mm Carbon Tubes


🚀 Pre-Flight & Tuning
Ensure the center of gravity is meticulously balanced at exactly 60mm from the wing root's leading edge.
Verify that the dual motors are rotating in opposite directions (CW and CCW) to effectively balance airflow and stabilize the airframe during flight.
Ensure all LiDAR arrays and Optical Flow sensors are unobstructed and calibrated for ground-texture recognition before attempting GPS-denied transitions.
